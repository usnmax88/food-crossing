Food Rush · Privacy policy

25 Labs LLC · in force from 12 November 2026

Food Rush is a game made by 25 Labs LLC. This page says what the game keeps, what it sends, and to whom. It covers the iPhone app and the web version.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts and we run no servers for it. We never receive your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

What the game keeps on your device

Your progress is saved only on your phone or in your browser: coins, stars, food cards, characters, missions, your best runs, the daily streak, and whether music, sound and buzz are on. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, deletes all of it.

Ads (iPhone app only)

The iPhone app shows video ads through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads. The web version shows none. To show ads and count them, Unity and its ad partners may receive:

  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you answer "Allow" when iOS asks about tracking;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate region can be told;
  • your device model, system version, language and network type;
  • which ads were shown and whether you watched or tapped them.

Unity handles this under its own policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. You can refuse tracking when iOS asks, or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.

Game Center (iPhone app only)

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game sends your score to Apple so it can show the leaderboard with your Game Center name. Apple handles this under its own policy: apple.com/legal/privacy. You can sign out of Game Center in your iPhone's Settings.

Purchases

Anything bought in the game is paid for through Apple's App Store. We never see your card or payment details.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text is published on this page with a new date.
